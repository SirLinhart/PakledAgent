Choose the plan of our Red Alert army. The summary above has one key=value group per line:
t=game seconds/seconds left. me=our family, en=enemy family.
cash=money we can spend, pw=power left over, harv=our harvesters.
bal=value we destroyed minus value we lost, army=our army value, assets=our assets value.
grp guard=, main=, raid=: units/strength of each group.
bld=our buildings by type and count.
en seen=1 if the enemy base was found, def=enemy defenses known, army=enemy army value, harv=enemy harvesters known, veh=1 if the enemy fields vehicles, age=seconds since an enemy building was in sight.
threat=none, minor or major attack on our base. plan=the plan in force.
The plans:
HOLD: stay home, grow the economy and the army, defend.
RAID: a small fast group hits the enemy's harvesters and economy where no defense covers them; the main army stays home.
PRESS: the main army attacks the enemy base once it is strong, and the raids go on.
Prefer RAID while the enemy economy is open, PRESS when our army clearly outweighs the enemy's, HOLD when our base is threatened or we are weaker. Never risk a lead late in the match.
